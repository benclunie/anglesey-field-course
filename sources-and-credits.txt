Anglesey coastal identification resources

Identification resources for C6002C17 - Environment and Geography Field Course at Harper Adams University. Explore coastal habitats and species during the week-long field course on Anglesey. Start with the short guides, then use the fuller references to check diagnostic features.

This public resource links directly to publisher-hosted guides. Download the PDFs you need before travelling; the linked resources otherwise require internet access.
Choose guides for the habitat and season. In autumn or winter, flower photographs and breeding-seabird sheets have limits; use vegetative plant characters and bird structure as well as colour.
Check more than one diagnostic feature. Record the date, place, habitat, size and useful photographs. Keep uncertain records at genus or group level, and ask a tutor to review them.
A spotter sheet is a shortlist, not a species-richness key. Do not count broad groups such as coral weeds or flat periwinkles as if they were resolved species.
Authors and publishers retain their rights in the linked material. This website hosts the resource directory and HAU design assets; it does not republish the identification PDFs.
